# 🌍 MCES INTERNATIONAL - Overseas Agency Management System

**MCES INTERNATIONAL** is a full-featured web platform built for an international travel agency, higher education consultancy, and skilled labor recruitment management firm. It features a modern, user-friendly client portal and a comprehensive **Admin Analytics Dashboard** for full operational control.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎓 1. Overseas Higher Education
- **University Admission & Visa Guidance:** Detailed information on top universities across the UK, USA, Canada, and Europe.
- **Scholarships & Consultancy:** Direct appointment scheduling with specialized education consultants.

### 💼 2. Skilled Labor Recruitment & Job Circulars
- **Overseas Job Postings:** Dynamic job listings for skilled/unskilled workers in countries like Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Romania, Japan, etc.
- **Job Category Badges & Details:** Shows salary details, position requirements, and quick "Apply Now" forms.

### ✈️ 3. Tour & Travel Packages
- **Domestic & International Tours:** Displays curated tour packages with itineraries, duration, and prices (e.g., Sri Lanka, India, Nepal, Cox's Bazar).
- **Direct Booking:** Easy package booking mechanism for customers.

### 📍 4. Live Passport & File Tracking
- **Real-time Status Tracking:** Clients can track their visa/passport processing status directly through unique IDs.
- **Status Lifecycle:** Live updates ranging from *Submitted*, *In Process*, to *Approved*.

### 📅 5. Appointment Booking & Consultation
- **Consultant Selection:** Users can choose specific departments (e.g., Overseas Education, Work Permit) and schedule appointments.
- **Instant Messaging & Contact:** Integrated contact forms, Google Maps location, direct WhatsApp, and phone support.

### 💬 6. Live Chat Console & Client Testimonials
- **Integrated Support:** Floating live chat console for immediate client assistance.
- **Success Stories & Reviews:** Highlights client reviews and success ratings.

---

## 🛠️ Admin Dashboard Capabilities

The platform comes with a powerful, real-time **Admin Panel** providing complete control over site data:

- **📊 Dashboard Analytics Overview:**
  - Bar charts and metrics comparing Tour Packages, Job Circulars, Blogs, and Passport Applications.
  - Pie chart representation of Passport Application statuses (*Approved*, *In Process*, *Submitted*).
- **🛂 Passport Management:** Add, update, or edit passport tracking progress for clients.
- **💼 Job Circulars Control:** Create, update, or remove job listings and worker requirements.
- **🗺️ Tour Package Management:** Manage destination packages, duration, pricing, and images.
- **🖼️ Content & Banner Slider:** Dynamically change homepage sliders, photo galleries, and blog articles.
- **💬 Live Chat Console:** View and respond to incoming chat requests from visitors.
- **📅 Appointment Tracking:** Manage all submitted appointment forms and schedule callbacks.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Prerequisites (Example)

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3/Tailwind CSS, JavaScript / React.js
- **Backend:** Node.js / Express.js / PHP / Laravel
- **Database:** MongoDB / MySQL
- **Charts & Visualizations:** Chart.js / ApexCharts
